## Published-clip policy

The garden's [clip-publishing skill](https://github.com/kriscendobot/garden/blob/main2/skills/minion-town-clip-publishing/SKILL.md) documents that published `<hash>.ocap.site` clips carry `img-src 'self' data:`, which admits the same-origin plates. This repository does not verify that policy against the live host; `tools/browser-check.mjs` reproduces it locally.
